## Hand-written programs for the transpiler. `node tools/strudel-ref/transpile-oracle.mjs` runs each through Strudel's transpiler
## (with the mini-notation, bare-samples and widget plugins, and the two embedded languages `mondo` and `mondolang`) with four sets
## of options and writes oracle.json; TranspileOracleTest compares the Kotlin transpiler with it, tree and context alike.
## Format: `### <name>` then the code; `### <name> !json` then one JSON string literal. `## ` lines are comments.
