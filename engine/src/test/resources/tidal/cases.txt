## Hand-written programs for the Haskell parser of `tidal`. `node tools/strudel-ref/hs-oracle.mjs` parses each with
## tree-sitter-haskell and evaluates it with hs2js over a symbolic scope; HsOracleTest compares the Kotlin parser and
## evaluator with the result, tree and value alike.
## Format: `### <name>` then the code; `### <name> !json` then one JSON string literal. `## ` lines are comments.
